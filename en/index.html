Park Liliowa

A ready-to-build investment project in Brzeszcze

Four terraced buildings, eight units, a final and binding building permit and complete documentation — an offer prepared for investors and developers looking for a project ready to build.

01
About the investment

Park Liliowa is a ready-to-build investment project located in Brzeszcze, on ul. Liliowa. The offer comprises a building plot of 1,591 m² together with complete design documentation and a final and binding building permit.

The project comprises four two-unit residential buildings in a terraced layout (segments A, B, C, D), providing a total of 8 dwelling units. It is aimed at investors and developers seeking a ready-to-build scheme in a quiet location with strong sales potential.

The plot sits among existing single-family housing, and just 600 m away lies the Natura 2000 site “Stawy w Brzeszczach” (Brzeszcze Ponds).

02
Architectural concept

The architectural concept of Park Liliowa is based on harmoniously fitting the terraced housing into the landscape of Brzeszcze. The buildings are designed on a polygonal footprint of two rectangles, giving an interesting, dynamic form and an optimal interior layout.

Each of the four segments is a structurally independent whole and contains two dwelling units. The two-storey buildings are topped with a symmetrical 35° gable roof, in line with the requirements of the local zoning plan (MPZP).

The layout provides a sense of privacy, natural daylight to the rooms and easy access to the green space around each home.

03
Scope of documentation

The offer includes the full package needed to start construction: the site development plan, the architectural and building design, technical designs (structure, plumbing and HVAC, electrical) and the administrative decisions obtained. The documentation has been prepared in accordance with the applicable regulations and the Brzeszcze local zoning plan (MPZP).

Designed parameters: building footprint 366.3 m² (23.02%), paved areas 503.37 m² (31.64%), biologically active area 721.33 m² (45.34%). The documentation also covers the necessary technical infrastructure, including the demolition and construction of a water-main section and the construction of four sealed stormwater tanks with a capacity of 10 m³ each.

The specific material choices leave the incoming investor room to tailor the finishing standard to their own sales strategy.

04
Location & potential

Park Liliowa is a project with strong potential in an attractive location. The plot lies on an internal municipal road — ul. Liliowa, with access to a public road (ul. Obozowa). The design provides 2 parking spaces in front of each segment (8 in total), private terraces and gardens, rooftop photovoltaics and a rainwater retention system used to water the gardens.

The site is not in a flood- or landslide-risk area, is not subject to heritage protection and does not require an environmental impact assessment.

Proximity to protected areas — Natura 2000 “Stawy w Brzeszczach” (0.60 km), the Soła River Valley (6.70 km) and the Dolna Soła area (4.74 km) — adds value for future home buyers. The project is a ready basis to start construction quickly and bring the product to the local housing market.
